Growth plan 2027

A short memo for Martí and Pol. Each section links to the full model.

The ask: to reach ~$3.1M of new ARR (60 logos) in 2027, I'd spend ~$350k on marketing and ~$125k on sales hires in 2027. Without changes, today's setup lands ~49 logos (~$2.5M).

  1. Goal setting. $3.1M means 60 contracts: 15 enterprise at ~$100k and 45 mid-market at ~$35k. That's ~280 opportunities, ~700 qualified leads, and ~7,000 people engaged across a 400–500 brand universe: ~15 per account, so we go deep, not wide.
  2. Diagnosis. The model breaks at the hand-off (AEs can call ~45 leads a month of ~49) and at closing (~30 open deals per AE vs 25). New leads go cold before they turn into deals.
  3. Model. The bridge from ~49 to ~60, and three plans. A lands ~60 of 60: more events and partners, which close better, plus a $30k buffer for extra hands. B ($310k as given) falls short at ~49. C ($550k) works if $200k moves to sales.
  4. Channels. Partners and events work today; digital is new and gets tested. EMEA and the US get different motions.
  5. 90 days. What I do first, and what I measure before money moves.

The first 90 days

  • Month 1: fix the hand-off (lead scoring, a hot-lead definition), lock the 400–500 target brands with Sales and the founders, one dashboard for sales and ad data.
  • Months 2–4: a 90-day ABM pilot on the top 170 accounts (~$25k): contact-level ads in the US, events and SDR follow-up in EMEA. Partner pilots go live.
  • Month 4: always-on runs in the winning story. The EMEA AE is hired in Q2; 2 SDRs start in Q3 to build 2028 pipeline.
  • Build first: I build what I can myself, and spend on targeted bets (a one-off US event, a freelance SEO test) once the early signals are clear.

How I built it

I built the model in Claude Code as one interactive page. Every number comes from the brief or is marked Assumed, and the tabs share one engine, so they stay in sync. I used Perplexity mainly to review market averages, like CAC benchmarks, and to run some of the ABM pilot assumptions. I also used Claude to make custom ad mockups, below.

One last thing

Not going to lie, I got caught up in some assumptions and correlations along the way, but I tried to be as specific as I could. Had a lot of fun building this though, and I wanted to share my first ad tests for Fermat with you. When Pol asked me if I had any connection to fashion, I forgot to mention my brief career as an 8 year old fashion model. Would have been a shame not to include these :D.

    • Leading indicators: partners activated · referred meetings · co-sell activity in open deals · opportunities per partner.
