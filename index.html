What runs at each stage

The colours match the funnel above. Each band has its own job, its own tactics and its own number to watch.

ProspectingAccounts and contacts
  • Build the list: fashion brands with $100M–$5B revenue, enterprise first.
  • Map the buying group: Heads of Design, Product and Digital. About 15 people per enterprise account, 6 per mid-market.
  • Rank titles: green to target, yellow when easier to reach, red to skip.

Watch: share of the list with a full buying group.

IntentNew leads and MQLs
  • ABM on LinkedIn to the named list, plus person-based ads to the same people.
  • Intent data to spot brands in market now.
  • Email to engaged contacts.
  • Organic: pick the podcast back up, with Heads of Design from target brands as guests.
  • Events and partner intros.
  • Small tests: Reddit and paid ChatGPT.

Watch: engaged accounts and cost per MQL.

Always onSQLs and opportunities
  • One evergreen contact-based ad journey: warm-up, then a theme that changes each quarter.
  • One site visit or two clicks moves a person to a "talk to us now" ad, and an SDR calls. Clicks trigger a call, not a score.
  • Once a deal opens, the ads follow it: the AE's face and a short video, then customer proof at the demo stage.

Watch: how much faster engaged accounts convert than cold ones.

Client marketingNew logos and beyond
  • Person-based ads to each new customer's team, timed with customer success onboarding.
  • Upsell and cross-sell campaigns: more teams, more seats, more brands in the group.
  • Turn wins into case studies and referrals that feed the top of the funnel.

Watch: expansion pipeline and referrals.

Today's engine closes about new logos a year. The gap is leads, sales capacity and timing.

Today's funnel vs. what 60 logos needs

"Needs" holds today's conversion rates and assumes steady volume all year. The lead gap tile adds two frictions: new-channel leads convert lower and only leads in by month close in 2027.

How I'd confirm it in 30 days

  • Pull conversion by source from the CRM: lead to opp, opp to won.
  • Measure cycle length by source and deal size.
  • Check AE workload: open opps per AE and time spent per deal.
  • Run 3 win and 2 loss interviews.
  • Agree the definition of a qualified lead and the handoff with the AEs.

05 · Paid enterprise

Paid enterprise: four channels, four jobs.

Paid isn't brand awareness here. Each channel has one job in the pipeline, and one number that decides whether it keeps its budget.

How scenario B's ~$180k paid enterprise budget splits.

1
LinkedIn

Warm the whole buying group

~70%~$130k of B

Why spend: Fermat's ICP is a list we can name: fashion brands with $100M–$5B revenue. LinkedIn is the only paid channel that targets them by account and by role, so the Head of Design, Product, Digital and IT all see Fermat before sales calls.

Audience: ~800 named accounts, 4–5 buyers each (~3,500 people)

Leading
% of target accounts reached, accounts with 2+ contacts engaged, buying-group coverage (decision maker / champion / gatekeeper)
Lagging
Opportunities from target accounts; engaged vs non-engaged conversion
Keep or cutKeep if engaged accounts convert to opportunities at 2x the rate of non-engaged ones.
2
Google search

Capture buyers already looking

~15%~$30k of B

Why spend: some brands are already comparing AI design tools. Search catches them at the moment of intent, on category terms ("AI fashion design software", "AI tech pack") and competitor names.

Leading
Impression share on category and competitor terms, click-through rate, branded search volume for "Fermat" (our awareness score: it rises when every other channel works)
Lagging
Demo requests and qualified leads from search, cost per qualified lead
Keep or cutCut competitor terms if their cost per qualified lead is 2x category terms.
3
Paid ChatGPT

Create champions inside ICP accounts

~5%~$10k test

Why spend: designers ask AI which tools to use. Ads there put Fermat in front of the people who use it daily, and a designer who's already using it is the warmest way into the buying group.

Leading
Sign-ups and trials, matched to company domain
Lagging
Sign-ups from ICP accounts → handed to sales and added to LinkedIn targeting; opportunities where a champion was already active
Keep or cutKeep if 10%+ of sign-ups come from ICP accounts after one quarter.
4
Reddit

Earn trust in designer communities

~3–5%$5–10k, mostly time

Why spend: designers trust peers over vendors. Being useful in fashion and design communities builds the reputation that makes champions recommend Fermat inside their company.

Leading
Mentions, upvoted answers, referral traffic from Reddit
Lagging
Free sign-ups from ICP companies
Keep or cutPause if there are no ICP sign-ups after one quarter.
Organic, no media budget

AI search visibility

Why: buyers now research in AI before they talk to sales. Being recommended in AI answers is free reach, and paid only works if the brand shows up when people check.

Measure: share of AI answers across 20–30 tracked prompts, quarter on quarter.

The link between them

  1. Designer channels (ChatGPT, Reddit) find champions
  2. Their company gets added to LinkedIn ABM
  3. The buying group warms up
  4. Sales starts the conversation with a champion already inside

Rule for moving budget: review monthly. Once a channel has 10+ qualified leads, move 10–20% of budget from the highest cost per opportunity to the lowest.
