04 · Channels

Where I'd put the money.

Ranked by how fast each one turns into opportunities the AEs want to work.

1

Partners and referrals

Highest win rate and larger deals. Agencies and platforms already sit inside the brands we want. Pay on wins, not on promises.

2

ABM on named accounts

The ICP of $100M–$5B brands is a list you can name. Build it, score it, and point every other channel at it.

3

Events: attend and host, no booths

Go where the buyers are, book meetings before the trip, and host small dinners for named accounts.

4

Paid enterprise, as an ABM layer only

Target the named list, retarget engaged accounts, and judge it on cost per opportunity, not clicks.

What I won't do

  • Broad awareness paid. Reach without a named account behind it doesn't turn into pipeline in 2027.
  • Booth sponsorships. High cost, badge scans, few buyers.
  • Mass cold outbound. It burns the list we need for ABM and the brand with it.
  • SEO as a 2027 pipeline channel. Worth starting, too slow to count on this year.

The rule for moving budget

Review monthly. Once a channel has 10+ qualified leads, move 10–20% of budget from the highest cost per opportunity to the lowest. Cut any channel with no opportunities after 2 quarters.
